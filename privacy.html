In short: Niyat has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your bookmarks, notes and settings stay on your device. To work, the app sends a few things to outside services: your location (for prayer times), the verse you ask about (for AI explanations), and ordinary download requests (for audio and wallpapers). We never sell data.

1. Who We Are

Niyat: Quran Transliteration ("Niyat", "we", "us") is developed by Al-Wijar Mawallil, an individual developer based in Davao City, Philippines. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

2. What We Do Not Collect

  • No account or sign-up: we do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking tools (for example, no Firebase Analytics and no Facebook SDK).
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell or rent your information.

3. Information Stored Only on Your Device

Your bookmarks, study notes, reading progress, lesson progress, prayer and notification settings, your last prayer-time location and your Premium status are saved locally on your device. We cannot see them. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

4. Information Sent to Outside Services

4.1 Location, for prayer times

If you allow location access, the app reads your device's location and sends its latitude and longitude to the AlAdhan prayer-times service (api.aladhan.com) to get your prayer timetable. The Qibla direction is calculated on your device. You can refuse or turn off location access in your device settings at any time; prayer times then cannot be calculated for your area.

4.2 Verse explanations

Most verse explanations are built into the app and work offline. When you ask for an AI-generated explanation, the app sends the verse reference and its text to an AI provider (Groq, Google Gemini or OpenRouter) to generate the answer. No personal information is included in these requests.

4.3 Audio, lessons and wallpapers

Recitation audio and Quran data are downloaded from Quran.com, alphabet audio from the Internet Archive (archive.org), and wallpapers from our own image server. Like any download, these servers receive technical information such as your IP address and device type in order to send the file.

4.4 Purchases

Niyat Premium is sold through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Apple or Google process your payment; we never see your card or billing details. The app only receives confirmation that your subscription is active.

4.5 Emails you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy that governs its handling of the information it receives.

5. Notifications

Prayer and Adhan notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No notification server is involved.

6. Children

Niyat is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Choices

  • Turn location access and notifications on or off in your device settings.
  • Avoid AI explanations if you prefer that no verse requests leave your device.
  • Delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the app.
  • Ask us to delete any emails you have sent us.

8. Security

All connections to the services above use encrypted HTTPS. Because we hold no personal data on our own servers, there is no user database to breach.

9. Changes to This Policy

If the app starts handling information differently, we will update this page and the "Last Updated" date before the change takes effect.

Questions?

We usually reply within a few days, insha'Allah.
