Common questions

Adhan or prayer alerts are not playing on Android

Some phones (Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, OnePlus, Huawei and others) stop apps in the background to save battery. Open Settings → Apps → Niyat → Battery and choose Unrestricted (or turn off battery optimisation), and allow Autostart if your phone has it. Also check that notifications and exact alarms are allowed for Niyat.

Prayer times look wrong for my city

Prayer times use your phone's location. Make sure location access is allowed for Niyat, then open the app again so it refreshes your timetable. Times can differ by a few minutes from your local mosque.

How do I restore Niyat Premium on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with, open the Support tab in Niyat and tap Restore.

How do I cancel Niyat Premium?

iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Niyat. Android: Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Niyat. Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

I found a mistake in a transliteration or translation

Jazakallah khair for telling us. Email us the surah and verse number (for example 2:2) and what looks wrong, and we will check and fix it.
